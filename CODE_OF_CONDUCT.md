# Code of Conduct

Tablet Workdesk is for practical, low-cost computing. Keep discussion focused,
respectful, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and specific.
- Assume device-specific failures need evidence.
- Share logs without private tokens, accounts, or personal files.
- Respect students, beginners, and low-budget users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Pressuring maintainers for unpaid urgent support.
- Encouraging unsafe public exposure of VNC/noVNC services.

Maintainers may close or remove issues that violate these expectations.
